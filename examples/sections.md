# Sections

The sections of your page, in order. Each one says what belongs in it, how to order it and how strictly to check it. Edit this file freely, or ask your agent ("add a Podcasts section", "no fact-checking on Videos", "put Essays first").

- `order`: how to rank items, in plain words. `impact` also groups the section into "Could change your life" and "Worth knowing".
- `checks`: `strict` fact-checks claims, judges arguments and drops weak or disputed pieces; `light` only cuts clickbait, promotion and paywalls; `off` lets everything on-topic in, summarized.
- `surprise: yes` makes a section of great picks from outside your interests; `max` caps how many items a section shows.

Items that fit no section are dropped and listed at the bottom of the page.

## News
Events that could change your money, work, health, rights or plans.
- order: impact
- checks: strict

## Articles
Ideas, explainers and research worth the time.
- order: importance to me
- checks: strict

## Essays
Arguments, critiques and criticism.
- order: importance to me
- checks: strict

## Videos
Talks, explainers and documentaries, judged from descriptions and reception.
- order: importance to me
- checks: strict

## Might surprise you
From outside your usual interests, chosen because they are good.
- surprise: yes
- checks: strict
