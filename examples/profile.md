# Reader profile

Who the paper is for and what they want. The triage, reader and scout agents judge every item against this file. Edit it freely, or ask your agent to. The "Learned from votes" section at the bottom is updated by `/feed` from the 👍/👎 buttons on the page.

## Wants

- **Example interest.** One line on what you want from it and what good looks like ("evidence-based health: sleep, exercise, nutrition; prefer pieces that cite real studies").

## Rejects

- **Too niche.** Anything that only matters to a small specialist group: release notes, setup guides for one tool, obscure sub-debates.
- **Launches and promotion.** Product announcements, "I built X", sponsored content, courses, company PR.
- **Clickbait.** Headlines that overpromise or hide the point.
- **Bad reception.** Pieces that comments or other outlets show to be fake, wrong or misleading.
- **Weak arguments.** Assertion without evidence, fallacies (slippery slope, straw man, false dilemma), cherry-picking.
- **Paywalled pages** where the content cannot be read.

## Balance

- Rules about the mix, for example: "Keep AI items to about a quarter of the paper."
